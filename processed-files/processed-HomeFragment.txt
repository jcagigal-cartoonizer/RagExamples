# 10) Important recommendation

Your current `MainActivityViewModel` is very large and still fragment-centric.  
For a clean Compose migration, I strongly recommend:

- keep `MainActivityViewModel` for global device/session flows
- move all screen-specific logic into screen ViewModels
- expose only:
  - `StateFlow<UiState>`
  - `SharedFlow<UiEvent>`

That will make the UI predictable and much easier to test.
