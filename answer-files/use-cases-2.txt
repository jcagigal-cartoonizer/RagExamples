## 1) Common enterprise use cases for RAG

RAG is useful whenever employees or customers need answers grounded in **company-specific, frequently changing, or hard-to-search knowledge**.

### A. Internal knowledge assistant
- Answers questions from policies, SOPs, wikis, handbooks, and intranet content
- Helps employees find “how do I…?” answers quickly
- Reduces dependency on subject-matter experts

### B. Customer support / contact center copilot
- Suggests responses to agents based on product docs, troubleshooting guides, and case history
- Powers self-service chatbots for customers
- Improves first-contact resolution and reduces handle time

### C. Sales enablement / account intelligence
- Retrieves product sheets, pricing rules, competitive battlecards, and customer history
- Helps sales reps prepare for meetings and draft follow-ups
- Supports proposal and RFP response generation

### D. Legal and compliance Q&A
- Searches contracts, regulations, policies, and audit evidence
- Helps teams answer “what does the policy say?” with citations
- Useful for clause comparison, obligation tracking, and policy interpretation

### E. HR and employee services
- Answers questions about benefits, leave, onboarding, travel, and payroll
- Automates HR helpdesk workflows
- Improves consistency in employee communications

### F. Engineering / IT operations assistant
- Retrieves runbooks, incident postmortems, architecture docs, and codebase knowledge
- Helps troubleshoot incidents and summarize logs/tickets
- Assists with internal developer support

### G. Research and analyst copilots
- Summarizes large document sets, market reports, and filings
- Helps analysts ask natural-language questions over internal research repositories
- Produces cited summaries and comparisons

### H. Procurement and supply chain
- Searches supplier contracts, SLAs, specs, and shipment records
- Helps compare vendors, terms, and compliance requirements
- Supports exception handling and sourcing decisions

### I. Finance and audit support
- Retrieves accounting policies, controls, and supporting documents
- Helps with close, audit prep, and variance explanations
- Supports policy-based Q&A with traceability

### J. Document-heavy workflows
- Insurance claims, loan processing, onboarding, KYC/AML, medical records review
- Extracts and grounds answers in forms, PDFs, emails, and scanned documents

## 2) Table: enterprise RAG use cases vs technologies used

| Use case | Typical data sources | Technologies commonly used | Why RAG fits |
|---|---|---|---|
| Internal knowledge assistant | Wikis, SharePoint, Confluence, PDFs, intranet | Document ingestion, chunking, embeddings, vector DB, LLM, citations | Answers must reflect internal, changing knowledge |
| Customer support copilot | KB articles, tickets, CRM notes, product docs | Hybrid search, reranking, vector DB, LLM, agent assist UI | Needs accurate, grounded responses and fast retrieval |
| Sales/RFP assistant | Battlecards, proposals, pricing docs, CRM, call notes | Semantic search, metadata filters, access control, LLM summarization | Reuses prior content and tailors responses to context |
| Legal/compliance assistant | Contracts, policies, regulations, case law | OCR, document parsing, clause extraction, vector search, citation grounding | Requires traceable answers and source references |
| HR assistant | HR policies, benefits docs, onboarding guides | Secure retrieval, role-based access, LLM, FAQ generation | Repetitive policy questions are ideal for retrieval |
| IT ops / developer assistant | Runbooks, incident logs, code docs, tickets | Log indexing, code-aware retrieval, reranking, agent workflows | Troubleshooting depends on internal, current context |
| Research/analyst copilot | Reports, filings, research notes, market data | Long-context retrieval, summarization, citation generation | Large document sets need grounded synthesis |
| Procurement assistant | Supplier contracts, SLAs, specs, emails | OCR, entity extraction, metadata search, vector DB | Helps compare terms and find relevant clauses |
| Finance/audit assistant | Policies, controls, ledgers, workpapers | Secure search, document QA, audit trail, citation support | Needs explainability and evidence-backed answers |
| Claims / case processing | Forms, scanned docs, emails, notes | OCR, entity extraction, workflow orchestration, LLM | RAG helps interpret unstructured case files |

### Common technology stack components
- **Data ingestion**: connectors to SharePoint, Confluence, Google Drive, Salesforce, ServiceNow, file systems
- **Document processing**: OCR, parsing, layout extraction, table extraction
- **Chunking and indexing**: splitting documents into retrievable chunks
- **Embeddings**: convert text into vectors for semantic retrieval
- **Vector database**: Pinecone, Weaviate, Milvus, FAISS, Elasticsearch/OpenSearch vector search
- **Hybrid retrieval**: combines keyword search + vector search
- **Reranking**: improves relevance of retrieved passages
- **LLM generation**: produces the final answer using retrieved context
- **Guardrails**: access control, PII redaction, hallucination checks, citation enforcement
- **Orchestration**: LangChain, LlamaIndex, Semantic Kernel, custom pipelines
- **Observability/evaluation**: tracing, answer quality metrics, retrieval evaluation

## 4) Practical examples of what RAG answers look like

### Example 1: HR
**Question:** “What is the parental leave policy for employees in Germany?”  
**RAG output:** Retrieves the Germany-specific HR policy, summarizes eligibility and duration, and cites the policy section.

### Example 2: Customer support
**Question:** “How do I reset a device that won’t connect to Wi‑Fi?”  
**RAG output:** Pulls the troubleshooting guide and known issues, then gives step-by-step instructions.

### Example 3: Sales
**Question:** “What are the approved discount ranges for enterprise customers in EMEA?”  
**RAG output:** Retrieves pricing policy, regional rules, and approval thresholds, then summarizes them with references.

### Example 4: Legal
**Question:** “Does this vendor contract include auto-renewal and what is the notice period?”  
**RAG output:** Extracts the relevant clause and returns the answer with the exact source text.

## 5) When enterprises should use RAG

RAG is especially valuable when:
- The knowledge base changes often
- Answers must be grounded in company documents
- You need citations or auditability
- The corpus is too large for prompt-only approaches
- Access control matters
- You want to reduce hallucinations

---

## 6) Common pitfalls to watch for

- Poor document chunking
- Weak metadata and filtering
- No reranking
- Outdated or duplicated content
- Missing access control
- No evaluation of retrieval quality
- Over-reliance on the LLM without citations
